About MedSafi

📊

Real FDA Data

Access millions of adverse event reports from the FDA's database spanning from 2004 to present. All data comes directly from official sources.

🔍

Easy to Search

Simply type a medication name and get instant access to reported uses, common adverse reactions, and detailed statistics.

💡

Educational Purpose

MedSafi is designed for educational purposes to help people understand medication information better. Always consult healthcare professionals.

Important Disclaimer

This tool provides information from the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS). The data represents voluntary reports and does not establish causation between medications and adverse events. This information should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult with your healthcare provider before making any decisions about your medications or health.

Data Sources

  • openFDA API: Official FDA adverse event reports
  • Coverage: Reports from 2004 to present
  • Update Frequency: Data is updated regularly by the FDA
